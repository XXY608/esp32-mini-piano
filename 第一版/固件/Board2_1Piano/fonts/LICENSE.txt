Generate-ChineseFont.py selects the interface characters in DisplayText.h,
the ASCII range, and all 6,763 GB2312 Han characters for imported song titles.
It preserves the original bitmaps. The screen renderer scales them at runtime.
This license notice applies to the font data, not to the surrounding firmware.
